# Worldbound Asset Prompt Library — Optional Future Art Production

These prompts are for creating replacements for the procedural SVG illustrations after the visual direction is approved. They are not required to run the game; `art.js` remains the offline fallback. Keep all generated assets original, trace their provenance, and match the location/element palette described in `WORLD_BUILDING.md`.

## Shared style prefix

> Original high-detail Chinese-inspired dark fantasy cultivation game concept art, readable silhouette at small UI size, cinematic atmospheric depth, deliberate brushwork, selective bioluminescence, luminous spirit qi, refined material textures, coherent five-element color language, strong focal point, no existing copyrighted characters or logos, no watermark, no text unless explicitly requested, horizontal 16:9 composition for location backgrounds, centered portrait framing for creature cards.

## Location backgrounds

1. **Village:** an ancient riverside cultivation village at sunrise, warm amber light over cream roofs, thin mist above rice fields, distant mountains, humble wooden gate and a quiet training yard, inviting but touched by mystery.
2. **Market:** a crowded night market of talisman stalls and herb merchants, violet-blue twilight, lantern pink and jade light, silk banners, apothecary glass, dense but readable silhouettes.
3. **Azure Cloud Sect:** white stone terraces above a sea of clouds, sky-blue banners, elegant stairways, small training figures, gentle shafts of light through cloud breaks.
4. **Whispering Forest:** deep teal canopy, glowing green herbs, enormous roots, floating spores and claw marks, clear moonlit path, hidden beast silhouette in the distance.
5. **Ancient Ruins:** broken sandstone towers with warm gold inscriptions, collapsed bridges, overgrown carvings, drifting dust, a forgotten kingdom's scale conveyed by tiny human silhouettes.
6. **Heaven's Peak:** storm-violet cliffs, distant lightning, high-altitude clouds, glowing runic fissures in the stone, dangerous but majestic vertical composition.
7. **Rotting Wastes:** olive and sickly lime fungal wilderness, dark wet soil, enormous spore growths and ash, glowing green veins under cracked ground, eerie beauty instead of gore.
8. **Abyssal Coast:** ink-blue sea cliffs, cyan bioluminescence beneath the waves, ancient bronze buoys and a broken ship, an immense silhouette below the horizon line.

## Creature portrait templates

Use the shared style prefix and add one of these subject descriptions:

1. **Bandit Chief:** a mortal road tyrant in weathered red scarf and dark lamellar coat, notched saber, suspicious eyes, grounded human scale, avoid cartoonish proportions.
2. **Flame-Tuft Spirit Beast:** fox-tiger guardian with ember mane, sleek paws, clever eyes, small wisps of fire rather than a giant flame explosion.
3. **Ruin Guardian:** spectral soldier in cracked bronze armor, spear held upright, faded royal sigil, pale blue ghost-light, mournful expression.
4. **Demon Cultivator:** dark-robed opponent with black-purple qi leaking from damaged meridians, ornate but worn seals, intimidating without gore.
5. **King Boar:** cart-sized earth boar with mossy bristles, cracked tusks, mud-caked armor plates, low frontal perspective implying a dangerous charge.
6. **Archespore:** ancient fungal titan with layered mushroom-like armor, glowing amber spore vents, fibrous roots, one clear weak point vulnerable to fire.
7. **Ursusumbra:** massive pale bear-like guardian with bone-white stone plates, dark cracks, heavy forelimbs, cold cavern backdrop.
8. **Frost Behemoth:** horned glacial titan with translucent blue ice plates, frozen vapor, clear horn silhouette, reflective but not over-glowing textures.
9. **Kraken:** abyssal multi-limbed spirit creature, ink-blue flesh, cyan runes, intelligent central eye and curling tentacles, a few bubbles and suspended debris.
10. **Ocean Leviathan:** colossal serpent-dragon under stormy ocean water, jade-cyan runes along scales, glowing eye, tiny ship silhouette for scale.

## UI icon direction

Create a family of simple high-contrast icons on transparent backgrounds: Dao paths, disciple roles, beast roles, factions, field research, herb, pill, spirit stone, fate, and warning. Use the palette as a semantic guide but pair each icon with a text label; never use color alone to signal a state.
